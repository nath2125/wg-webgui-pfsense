Traffic shaping — Local priority over WireGuard

        —
      

      

        Prioritises Local traffic — your LAN and all VLANs, i.e. anything
        local heading out — over the WG class on the WAN uplink under
        congestion. WG still gets the full pipe when local traffic is idle. Manages its
        own limiters/queues only.
      


      

        
        
        
        
      


      

        
        
      

      

      


      

        

          
Shaped rules 

          
        

        

          The rules below currently carry the limiters — this is the real status. Set any rule to
          Local, WG or Bulk, or detach it.
          Search to find other pass rules to add — including inbound WAN rules (a port forward or
          reverse proxy), which are the only place traffic the internet initiated can be shaped.
        

        

          WG is the class you want held back, not every rule that touches
          WireGuard. Put always-on site-to-site endpoints (an offsite backup node and its VMs)
          there; leave interactive peers — a phone or laptop on the VPN — on Local,
          since throttling them costs latency without freeing real bandwidth. Bulk
          sits below both for transfers that should yield to everything.
